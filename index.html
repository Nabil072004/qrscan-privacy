QR Scan – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

In short

  • No account, no ads and no tracking of what you scan.
  • Your scan history, "My QR" details and settings stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • Codes are read on your phone. The app does not upload what you scan, your photos or camera pictures.
  • Google's ML Kit, the component that reads codes, may send technical diagnostics to Google (see section 4).

This policy explains how the "QR Scan" Android app (the "app") handles information. The app is provided by its developer (the "developer", "we"). You can reach us at yourmonostick@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your phone

The app keeps the following only in its private storage on your device:

WhatWhyHow to remove it
Scan history: the content of codes you scan (for example a link, text, Wi-Fi name and password, or contact details) and the time of the scanSo you can find a past scan againSettings → Clear history, delete a single scan, or turn off "Save scan history"
"My QR" details: the name, phone, email and company you typeTo show your own contact QR codeEdit or clear the fields in My QR, or clear the app's data
App settings: sound, vibration, theme and colour choicesTo remember your preferencesClear the app's data

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this. The app does not create a user account and does not send this information to us or to any other server. App data is excluded from Android cloud backup.

If a code you scan contains a Wi-Fi password and history is on, that password is saved in your history on your phone. Turn off "Save scan history" if you prefer that scans are not stored.

2. Camera and photos

  • Camera. The camera is used only while the scanner is open, to find QR codes and barcodes. Camera images are analysed on your phone, are not saved and are not sent anywhere.
  • Gallery. If you choose "From gallery", the picture you select (using Android's photo picker) is read once on your phone to look for a code. The app does not get access to the rest of your photos.
  • Saving and sharing. When you tap "Save image" or "Share" on a QR code you created, the image is saved to your Pictures folder or handed to the app you choose. This happens only when you ask for it.

3. Links and other content in codes

A QR code can contain anything. The app shows you what a code contains and only opens ordinary web links (http or https), and only after you tap "Open". The website you visit is run by someone else and has its own privacy practices. We recommend checking a link before you open it.

4. Third-party components

Google ML Kit. The app uses Google's ML Kit barcode scanning to read codes. Reading happens on your device. According to Google, ML Kit may send technical diagnostics and usage analytics to Google over HTTPS, such as device information (manufacturer, model, Android version), app information (package name and version), performance metrics, error codes, and an identifier for the app installation. Google states that it does not transfer this data to third parties. See ML Kit data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play services. When you tap Scan, the camera screen may be provided by the Google Code Scanner, part of Google Play services on your phone. It is operated by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

The app contains no advertising, no other analytics or crash-reporting tools and does not use the advertising ID.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with anyone. We do not receive your scans, photos, contact details or settings.

6. Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Security

Data in the app is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a normal, unmodified Android phone, and it benefits from the encryption of your phone's storage. Please keep your phone locked with a screen lock.

8. Your choices and rights

You control everything the app stores: you can delete scans, clear history, edit or remove your "My QR" details, or uninstall the app. Because we do not collect your personal information on any server, we hold no data about you to access, correct or delete. For diagnostics handled by Google, please use Google's privacy tools or contact Google.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above, and within the app where appropriate.
